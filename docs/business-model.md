# Business Model: Paper Products Machine Operators Plant Scheduling and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-8143`
- ISCO-08: `8143`
- Occupation: Paper Products Machine Operators
- Social impact: worker-safety, industrial-continuity, supply-chain-resilience

## Customer

- paper-converting plant operators / box-making, bag-making and
  tissue-converting line contractors
- independent paper-converting crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- production-run/inventory/progress-record logging
- raw-paper/converting-materials supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to plant safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a machine-operation-execution decision (e.g.
  deciding to proceed with a specific converting/cutting/folding run), ever
- no direct finalization of a plant-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring the plant safety cleared), ever
- no override of a plant safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (roller entanglement, blade injury, crush
  hazard, equipment condition) always route to human sign-off, regardless
  of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
